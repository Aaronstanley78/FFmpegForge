== FSR2.glsl: FFmpeg Forge temporal upscaler (FSR 2 / FSR 3 types) ==
Written for FFmpeg Forge following the design of AMD FidelityFX Super Resolution 2
(GPUOpen, MIT): motion estimation from the video, history reprojection, YCoCg variance
clipping and accumulation. It reuses the FSR 1 EASU and RCAS passes of FSR.glsl
(AMD, MIT license above). AMD's FSR 2/3/4 SDK binaries are not included.
